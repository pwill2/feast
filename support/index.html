Common questions

The widget isn’t showing or updating.

Add it from the lock screen (long-press → Customize → the area below the clock) or the home screen (long-press → +). Verses change on the schedule you set in the app’s Settings (default: a new verse every 3 hours, 6am–10pm).

The verse doesn’t change outside certain hours.

That’s by design — outside your active hours the widget holds the last verse of the day, then advances when the next window opens.

How do I change how often it rotates?

Open the app → Settings → “New verse every.” You can pick 1, 2, 3, 6, or 12 hours, set your active hours, and reshuffle the order anytime.

Is my data collected?

No. Feast has no account, no analytics, and no tracking, and it stores everything on your device. Reading scripture works entirely offline. See the Privacy Policy.

Full Feast subscription

What does the subscription include?

The Book of Mormon is free, forever, with no purchase required. Full Feast adds the Bible, the Doctrine and Covenants, and the Pearl of Great Price, lets you choose which volumes and books your widget draws from, and unlocks Scripture Gate.

How do I cancel?

On your device: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Feast. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so cancellation has to happen there rather than in the app.

I subscribed but the extra volumes aren’t showing.

Open the paywall (Settings → “Upgrade to Full Feast”) and tap Restore. That re-checks your purchase with Apple. If it still doesn’t unlock, email me and I’ll sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me — use reportaproblem.apple.com. I have no access to your payment details or purchase history.

Scripture Gate

What is it?

Scripture Gate puts a verse in front of apps you’d rather not open without thinking. You choose the apps, and you choose what opens them — a number of verses read, chapters finished, or minutes spent. Meet it and those apps stay open for the rest of the day; the gate returns tomorrow.

It is friction, not a lock. You can turn it off in the app at any time.

Why does it ask for Screen Time access?

Placing a verse in front of another app requires Apple’s Screen Time frameworks — there is no other way to do it. The permission is used for nothing else. Feast never learns which apps you chose: iOS keeps that selection in a form the app cannot read or transmit.

The shield isn’t appearing.

Check that Screen Time access is still allowed (Settings → Scripture Gate), that at least one app is selected, and that you haven’t already met today’s target — once you do, the gate stays open until tomorrow.

Scroll Mode

Scroll Mode is a scripture feed built for the way you already scroll — one verse per card, in order or shuffled, or following a track gathered around a theme like faith, hope, or deliverance. It also picks up where you left off.
