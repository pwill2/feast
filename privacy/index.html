Feast — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026
Home · Support

What Feast does with your information: nothing.

We do not collect any data

Feast has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections. Every verse and all settings are stored on your device.

  • No personal information is collected, stored on a server, or transmitted.
  • No usage or analytics data is gathered.
  • No third-party SDKs for tracking, ads, or analytics are included.
  • No device identifiers are read for tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of data locally, in an app group shared between the app and its widget, solely so the app can function:

  • Your rotation preferences (interval and active hours).
  • A random number (“shuffle seed”) that determines your personal verse order.

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you delete the app.

Children’s privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.
