Feast — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

What Feast collects about you: nothing.

We do not collect any data

Feast has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted to us or to anyone else. We could not identify you if we wanted to.

  • No personal information is collected, stored on a server, or transmitted.
  • No usage or analytics data is gathered.
  • No third-party SDKs for tracking, ads, or analytics are included.
  • No device identifiers are read for tracking.
  • We operate no servers. There is nowhere for your data to go.

Network use

Every verse ships inside the app, so reading scripture works entirely offline — in airplane mode it behaves exactly as it does anywhere else.

The app itself makes no network requests. There are two exceptions, both handled by Apple rather than by us:

  • Subscriptions. If you subscribe to Full Feast, Apple’s App Store handles the purchase, the price shown, and renewals. We never see or store your payment details, and we receive no personal information about you from Apple.
  • Links you tap. Sharing a verse, or opening the support, terms, or App Store links, hands off to another app on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores a modest amount of data locally — some of it in an app group shared between the app, its widget, and its Screen Time extensions — solely so the app can function:

  • Your rotation preferences (interval and active hours), and which volumes or books the widget draws from.
  • A random number (“shuffle seed”) that determines your personal verse order.
  • Verses you like, save, or share, with the date you did so.
  • Where you left off reading, and a daily count of verses read and time spent, kept so the app can show you your own reading history.
  • Your Scripture Gate settings, and whether today’s gate is open.
  • The date the app was first opened, used to show the correct launch pricing.

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you delete the app. There is no backup of it on our side, because there is no our side.

Screen Time and Scripture Gate

Scripture Gate is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, it uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity) to show a verse before apps you choose.

Feast never learns which apps you selected. iOS holds that selection in an opaque form the app cannot read, list, or transmit — by design, this is how Apple built it. Feast does not read your usage statistics either; it does not request the entitlement that would allow it to. The app knows only whether the gate is currently open and how much you have read toward opening it.

You can turn Scripture Gate off at any time in the app.

Children’s privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date. If a future version of the app ever sends anything off your device, this page will say so plainly, and before it happens.
